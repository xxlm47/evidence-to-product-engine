# Organic Marketing Repurposer

Turn a finished book into useful promotional material without spam.

Generate:
- 10 short tips
- 10 common mistakes
- 10 FAQs
- 10 problem/solution posts
- 5 short-video scripts
- 5 educational threads/posts
- 5 free sample excerpts

Every item must provide standalone value. Avoid deceptive claims, fake scarcity, fake testimonials, engagement bait that misrepresents the product, and community spam. Respect each platform's rules.
