# Topic Discovery

Generate practical nonfiction topic candidates from a user-provided domain.

For each candidate identify:
- Specific reader
- Specific problem
- Search intent
- Likely urgency
- Evidence to collect
- Existing competitor types
- Possible differentiation
- Evergreen/time-sensitive status
- Companion products
- Series expansion

Do not declare a winner. Rank only by documented evidence if the user explicitly requests prioritization, and show the evidence behind each placement.

Reject ideas that depend on fabricated expertise, copyrighted material, prohibited products, or unverifiable claims.
