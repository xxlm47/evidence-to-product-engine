# Launch Checklist

## Product
- [ ] Final manuscript
- [ ] Cover
- [ ] Description
- [ ] Keywords
- [ ] Categories
- [ ] Price
- [ ] Companion product

## Distribution
- [ ] KDP listing prepared
- [ ] Gumroad package prepared
- [ ] Free sample prepared

## Organic promotion
- [ ] 10 short tips
- [ ] 10 FAQs
- [ ] 10 mistakes
- [ ] 10 educational posts
- [ ] 5 short-video concepts
- [ ] 5 community-help answers where promotion is allowed

## Measurement
Record:
- Publication date
- Price
- Sales
- Revenue
- Reviews
- Reader questions
- Traffic observations
- Refunds/complaints
- Update opportunities

## Reinvestment rule
Do not buy tools simply because they exist. A paid expense must be funded by existing revenue and have a clear reason to improve revenue, quality, or production capacity.
