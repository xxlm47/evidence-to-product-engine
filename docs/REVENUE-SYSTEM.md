# Revenue System

The engine is designed around a simple loop:

`Demand -> Evidence -> Gap -> Product -> Publish -> Distribution -> Measure -> Improve -> Next Product`

## 1. Demand

Find an existing problem before writing. Search Amazon, Google, YouTube, Reddit, forums, official documentation, and other public sources.

## 2. Evidence

Record URLs, dates, search terms, recurring questions, competitor products, and reader complaints. Do not treat a single post as proof of market demand.

## 3. Gap

Cluster recurring complaints and missing information. A gap should be specific enough to become a useful promise.

## 4. Product

Create the smallest useful product that solves the gap. Start with a practical ebook and only create companion products when they add real value.

## 5. Publish

Prepare KDP and/or Gumroad packages. Review rights, accuracy, metadata, platform requirements, and AI disclosure requirements before publication.

## 6. Distribution

Start with free organic channels. Make educational posts, short videos, checklists, samples, and answers that are useful on their own.

## 7. Measure

Track publication date, price, sales, revenue, reviews, reader questions, traffic indicators available to you, and update opportunities.

## 8. Improve

Improve weak descriptions, confusing chapters, missing sections, outdated information, and companion products based on evidence.

## 9. Expand

Use legitimate adjacent problems to build a catalog. Do not clone the same book with superficial wording changes.

## $0 guardrail

Before revenue exists, reject expenses for software, paid APIs, advertising, domains, hosting, outsourcing, premium assets, and other optional services when a free alternative is adequate.

Transaction fees that occur only after a sale are different from startup costs, but every platform's current terms must be checked before use.
