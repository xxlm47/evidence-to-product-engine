# Engine Specification

## Goal
Turn a validated problem into a review-ready digital product package while keeping startup cost at $0 and requiring human approval before publication.

## Pipeline contract

Each stage must consume explicit inputs and produce named artifacts. A stage must never claim work was performed unless its artifact contains evidence or an explicit TODO.

1. TOPIC — topic + audience
2. MARKET SCAN — sources, dates, search observations
3. DEMAND EVIDENCE — recurring demand signals
4. COMPETITOR ANALYSIS — comparable products and positioning
5. REVIEW MINING — complaints, requests, missing information
6. GAP DETECTION — clustered opportunities
7. OPPORTUNITY REPORT — evidence-backed go/no-go recommendation
8. OUTLINE — reader outcome and chapter plan
9. BOOK CREATION — draft manuscript
10. FACT CHECK — claims and source ledger
11. EDIT — clarity, usefulness, consistency
12. PACKAGING — metadata, cover brief, bonus assets
13. DISTRIBUTION — KDP/Gumroad-ready package
14. FREE TRAFFIC — platform-specific content queue
15. METRICS — sales and feedback log
16. IMPROVE/UPDATE — evidence-driven revision
17. NEXT PRODUCT — adjacent validated opportunity

## Hard gates

- No evidence => no validation.
- No validation => no full manuscript.
- Unverified factual claims => not ready.
- Rights/licensing uncertainty => not ready.
- Platform-policy uncertainty => not ready.
- Human approval is mandatory before publication.

## Provider neutrality

The core repository must not require one paid AI provider. AI-assisted steps may be performed manually, through an available free service, through a local model, or through a future adapter.

## Revenue discipline

Optimize for useful products and repeatable catalog growth, not volume for its own sake. Never guarantee sales or fabricate market evidence, reviews, credentials, or sources.
