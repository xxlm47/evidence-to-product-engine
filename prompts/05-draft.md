# Practical Drafting

Write only from the approved outline and verified research.

Style:
- Direct
- Concrete
- Beginner-friendly when appropriate
- Step-by-step
- Useful before impressive

Use examples and checklists. Do not invent sources, quotes, case studies, statistics, user experiences, or product features. Mark anything requiring verification as [VERIFY].

Do not pad the manuscript to hit a word count. The target is useful information, normally 10,000–30,000 words for a full guide unless the subject calls for another length.
