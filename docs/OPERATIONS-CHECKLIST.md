# Operations Checklist

## Before research

- [ ] Topic is specific.
- [ ] Target reader is specific.
- [ ] Desired outcome is measurable.
- [ ] Startup cost remains $0.

## Before writing

- [ ] Demand evidence recorded.
- [ ] Competitors recorded.
- [ ] Review complaints/questions recorded.
- [ ] Gap is explicit.
- [ ] Opportunity report says PROCEED.

## Before publishing

- [ ] Every important factual claim checked.
- [ ] Sources and dates recorded.
- [ ] Copyright/trademark issues reviewed.
- [ ] AI disclosure requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Manuscript edited.
- [ ] Metadata checked.
- [ ] Cover rights checked.
- [ ] KDP/Gumroad requirements checked.
- [ ] Human approval recorded.

## After launch

- [ ] Sales/revenue logged.
- [ ] Reviews/questions captured.
- [ ] Problems converted into update candidates.
- [ ] Related-product opportunities reviewed.

## Stop conditions

Stop and investigate if evidence is missing, sources cannot be verified, the product would be mostly filler, or a proposed expense is required before revenue exists.
