# Market Research

Investigate whether a topic has observable demand.

Collect:
- Marketplace search evidence
- Search suggestions
- Public questions
- Recent tutorials/discussions
- Competing books/products
- Publication dates
- Pricing observations
- Repeated reader problems

For each source record URL, source name, date accessed, and the exact observation used.

Do not treat search volume estimates, reviews, bestseller status, or social engagement as guaranteed sales. Distinguish signals from proof.
